- **Citation capsule** (40-60 words): Google requires verification before a business profile can rank consistently in local results. The fastest method is video verification, where you record your location or equipment. Until verified, your profile will not appear reliably in the map pack, and you cannot respond to reviews, publish posts, or access performance data.

### H2: Choose the Right Primary Category (250-300 words)
- **Answer-first**: Your primary category is the strongest relevance signal Google reads from your profile. Choosing a broad category when a specific one exists costs you visibility.
- Cover: primary vs. secondary categories, how to find the right one (search your service + city and see what the top 3 listings use), common mistakes (using "General Contractor" when "HVAC Contractor" exists), when to add secondary categories.
- **Citation capsule** (40-60 words): Choosing the most specific primary category on your Google Business Profile is the single fastest way to improve relevance. A general contractor who primarily does HVAC work should list "HVAC Contractor," not "General Contractor." Google matches your primary category against the searcher's query before considering any other profile signal.
- **Key stat**: GBP signals (including category) account for 32% of local ranking factors (Whitespark 2026).

Complete profiles receive more direction requests and website clicks than incomplete ones, because Google can confidently show them for a wider range of queries.

### FAQ Section (4-5 items)
1. **Is Google Business Profile free?** Yes. Claiming, verifying, and optimizing your profile costs nothing. Google charges only if you run ads.
2. **How long does it take for changes to show up?** Most edits appear within 24-48 hours. Verification takes 5-10 days by postcard or a few days by video.
3. **Does my Google Business Profile affect my website's organic ranking?** Indirectly. GBP signals drive local pack rankings (a separate system from organic). A strong profile builds brand prominence, which can help branded organic queries over time.
4. **How do I get more reviews without being pushy?** Ask in person right after a completed job, when satisfaction is highest. Send a follow-up SMS with a direct review link. Never offer incentives for reviews.
5. **Can I rank in the map pack for cities I don't have an office in?** Yes, if you are a service-area business. Define your service area in your GBP settings and build a unique landing page for each city on your website. Proximity still matters: ranking in distant cities requires stronger prominence signals.
